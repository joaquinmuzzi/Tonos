# Code of Conduct

orfeo follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to everyone who takes part in the project: in issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions and any other space related to orfeo.

In short: be respectful and kind, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and
level of experience, and focus on what is best for the project and its community. Harassment,
insults, discrimination and personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks this code of conduct, write to the maintainer,
Joaquín Muzzi, at **joaquin.hola.muzzi@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately and as quickly
as possible, and the person who reports is protected from retaliation.

The maintainer decides on the consequences following the enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant: from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project.
