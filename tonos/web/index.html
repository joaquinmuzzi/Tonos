How reliable is this?
  • Tempo and beats: very good for pop, rock and electronic music with drums. The typical error is giving double or half the tempo. Much worse for music without drums, live recordings or rubato. Turn on the click to check it by ear.
  • Meter: the hardest part. It counts the beats between downbeats, and downbeats fail more often than beats (about 1 in 5). 3/4 and 6/8 cannot be told apart automatically.
  • Key: in published tests, the model gets the exact key right for 7 to 8 songs out of 10. When it fails, it almost always confuses it with the relative key (C major ↔ A minor) or with a fifth. It gives a single key per song, and modal music (Dorian, Mixolydian…) is forced into major or minor.
  • Chords: correct about 78 % of the time for major/minor, less with sevenths and unusual chords. That is why they start in “simple” mode. Basses and inversions often fail.
  • Repertoire: the models were trained mostly on pop and rock sung in English. Folk, tango and other Latin American genres may give worse results.

Models: Beat This! (CPJKU, 2024), S-KEY (Deezer, 2025) and Consonance-ACE (Poltronieri, Serra and Rocamora, 2025), plus librosa for the control tempo and the tuning.
